Let Opus find what matters in your data and recommend what to do about it.

Recommendations launched on guest feedback. This update expands what feeds into them, including Audits, Checklists, Knowledge Checks, and other Opus data like Ask Opus.

Recommendations already assign training, update existing content, and create new training. This update adds actions like creating a Task and recognizing someone on the team.

Not every Recommendation is a problem to fix. When a team member gets praised again and again in guest reviews, Opus can put that in front of leadership.
