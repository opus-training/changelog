Score the photos your team submits through Checklists and Check-ins against your own standard.

Admins upload a reference image and describe what makes a submission acceptable. Opus reviews each photo a team member submits and scores it against that standard.

This saves Managers time because they no longer review every photo by hand, and the standard holds at every location.
