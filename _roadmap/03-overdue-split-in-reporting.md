title: "Reporting now splits Overdue from Incomplete"
status: just-launched
tags: [Reporting]
audience: [Admins, Managers, Trainers]
---

Past Due is now broken out in Reporting, so your team can tell assignments that are actually late from assignments that simply aren't due yet.

Find it in the dashboard: Reporting > Organization > Reach & Engagement, and alongside Assignment completion in the Location tab.

[See it in Reporting](https://dashboard.opus.so/reporting?tab=location)
