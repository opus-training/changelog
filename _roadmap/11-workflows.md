Let Opus act on what happens, so nothing falls through the cracks.

Workflows are an additional automation layer in Opus. Something happens, and Opus does what you told it to do.

Send a report the moment a Checklist is completed. Text a Manager when a scheduled window gets missed. Admins decide exactly what should happen when something important occurs, and Opus takes it from there.
