Score how each location is really doing, and turn what you find into work that gets done.

An Audit is a scored inspection that checks your operations against your standards. Admins set the passing score, and any flagged step can create a follow-up Task so the problem gets owned, fixed, and signed off.

Use them for location walkthroughs, safety checks, and food safety compliance, all in the same app your team already uses for training.
