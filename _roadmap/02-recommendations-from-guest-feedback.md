Turn guest reviews into training that fixes the problem behind them.

Opus reads your guest reviews, finds the patterns behind your ratings, and turns them into a prioritized list of training actions for the locations affected. Connect Google Business Profile, Ovation, or Tattle, or upload a CSV.

Every Recommendation suggests one action: assign training you already have, update content that isn't landing, or create something new. Nothing reaches your team until you approve it, and Opus tracks whether guest feedback improves afterward.
