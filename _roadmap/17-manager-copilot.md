Give Managers one place to start the day, and something to lean on for the hard calls.

Manager Copilot brings Ask Opus, Opus assistants, and Recommendations into a single daily tool. It shows Managers what they need to know and what deserves their focus, then helps them get the work done.

Managers make decisions day to day without having the complete picture. With this update, Opus understands your standards, priorities, and context well enough to help them make the right decision.
