Manage compliance training across every location from one place.

Opus already carries premium compliance content in the [Marketplace](https://help.opus.so/en/articles/7135879-marketplace-overview), including [Food Safety](https://help.opus.so/en/articles/8183210-food-safety-training) certification through Always Food Safe and [Sexual Harassment Prevention](https://help.opus.so/en/articles/6116200-sexual-harassment-prevention-training) training built with Fox Rothschild LLP.

This work makes that content easier for Admins to assign, track, and prove across locations.
