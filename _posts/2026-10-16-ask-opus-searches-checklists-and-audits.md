date: 2026-10-16

---

Ask Opus now answers from your checklists, audits and check-ins, not just your courses and resources.

- **Find the procedure you wrote** — ask about opening and closing steps, line checks or food-safety checks, and Ask Opus answers from the checklist or audit that covers it.
- **Every part of the step counts** — Ask Opus reads titles, descriptions, sections, step text, help text, answer choices and what to do when a step fails.
- **See where the answer came from** — answers cite the checklist, audit or check-in they came from, just like courses and resources.
- **Only what you can already see** — people get answers only from items already in their library. Submitted answers and results are never included.
