---
title: "Timed video annotations"
date: 2026-05-15
kind: feature
tags: [Feature, Courses]
---

Annotations on a training video can now appear only during a specific part of playback.

- **Time-bound callouts** — set an annotation to appear during a specific part of playback, instead of staying on screen for the whole video.
- **The right detail at the right moment** — call out exactly what matters while it is on screen.
