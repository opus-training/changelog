Profile now lives behind a person icon in the top-right corner of the mobile app, in the same place for everyone.

- **One less tab for Trainees** — the Profile tab is gone from the bottom bar; tap the person icon at the top right of Home, Library, Messages, or Ask Opus instead, including when you're offline.
- **The same spot for Managers and Admins** — the icon you already used on Home and Team now also appears on Library, Messages, and Ask Opus.
- **A clearer Home for Managers and Admins** — Home now splits into a Team section, for check-ins to facilitate, overdue team training, and your locations, and a Training section for your own Knowledge Check and assignments.

*Available in the Opus mobile app. If you don't see it yet, update to the latest version.*
