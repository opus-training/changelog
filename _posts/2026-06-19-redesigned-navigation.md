---
title: "Redesigned navigation"
date: 2026-06-19
kind: feature
tags: [Feature, Dashboard]
---

The web app's left-hand navigation is now a compact rail of your main areas, with a second panel for the pages inside.

- **Compact rail** — Home, Training, Operations, Reporting, People and Messages, with a second panel that lists the pages inside whichever area you are in.
- **Settings gets its own navigation** — and the organization switcher and the "New" button now sit in the rail.
- **More room to build** — the navigation collapses on its own while you are working in the content builder, so the builder gets the full width of the screen.
- **One removal** — as part of the new layout, the summary counts that used to sit above the training library list have been removed.
