title: "Location in training copy"
date: 2026-09-08
kind: feature
tags: [Feature, Training, Courses]
---

You can personalize training with `{% raw %}{{location}}{% endraw %}`, the same way as `{% raw %}{{name}}{% endraw %}` and `{% raw %}{{company}}{% endraw %}`.

- **The learner’s location, in the course.** Learners see their location filled in automatically.
- **No blank if they don’t have one.** People without a location see “your location” instead of a gap.
