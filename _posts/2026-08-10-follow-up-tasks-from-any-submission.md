---
title: "Follow-up Tasks from any submission"
date: 2026-08-10
kind: feature
tags: [Feature, Tasks, Audits]
---

When a submission comes back with something that needs fixing, you can now raise a tracked follow-up Task straight from it, on Checklists as well as Audits, on the dashboard and in the app.

[Tasks Overview](https://help.opus.so/en/articles/16311874-tasks-overview) · [How to Set Up and Manage Tasks](https://help.opus.so/en/articles/16311589-how-to-set-up-and-manage-tasks)

- **Checklists, not just Audits.** Any completed Checklist submission can now turn into a follow-up Task, so the fix gets an owner and a due date instead of a verbal reminder.
- **Raise one on the spot.** The Follow-up Tasks tab is now there on every submission you can act on, rather than appearing only once a Task exists. Set the owner, the due date, what proof it needs and whether it has to be reviewed as you create it.
- **An owner every time.** A follow-up Task can no longer be saved without an assignee, so nothing is raised and then forgotten.
- **Wherever you review.** The same flow on the phone for Managers working on the floor, and in the dashboard for Admins reviewing submissions after the fact.

*Available to Admins and Managers, in businesses using Audits or Checklists.*
