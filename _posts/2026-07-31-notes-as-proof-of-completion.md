---
title: "Notes as proof of completion"
date: 2026-07-31
kind: feature
tags: [Feature, Tasks, Audits]
---

When a photo won't tell the story, a task can now require a written note instead.

- **A third kind of proof** — alongside a photo and a video, any task can now ask for a written note, so "explain what you did" counts as proof for the fixes a camera can't show.
- **Written where the work is** — the person assigned writes the note on the task itself, on the app or in the dashboard, and the task can't be submitted or signed off until it's there.
- **Part of the record** — the note is saved to the task's history, so a reviewer can see what was written and when it changed.

*Available to organizations using audits.*
