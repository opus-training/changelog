### New features

- **Redesigned module screen** — the mobile module screen has a new step-by-step
  timeline showing exactly where a trainee is in their training, a simpler header
  with the due date and progress at a glance, and a single button to start or pick
  up where they left off.
- **Duplicate a section** — when building check-ins, audits, and task lists, you can
  now copy an entire section — steps, conditional groups, and triggers — in one
  click, instead of rebuilding it by hand.
- **Required comments on low course ratings** — when a trainee rates a course three
  stars or fewer, they're now asked to say why before submitting, so the feedback
  you get back is specific enough to act on.

### Bug fixes

- Fixed an issue where invitation and scheduled report emails could silently fail to
  send for several hours.
- Fixed an issue where uploaded PDF and media training resources weren't searchable
  or citable in Ask Opus.
- Fixed an issue where QR codes in training content failed to load.
- Fixed sorting on the Reviews page and on the "Include in Knowledge Check" column in
  your library — clicking those column headers did nothing.
- Fixed an issue where locations that belong to a brand but not a region were missing
  from reporting filters and completion grids.
- Fixed an issue where "Average time to complete" showed as unavailable on a course or
  module's Reporting tab.
- Fixed an issue where Region, Brand, and Franchise columns could show up empty on the
  Locations table for organizations that don't use them.
- Fixed an issue where marking a large batch of trainings complete could time out.
- Fixed an issue where columns in reporting completion grids rearranged themselves
  after expanding a module's content.
- Fixed an issue where resources could appear to vanish from a course when switching
  between courses quickly.
- Sped up the initial load of the skill and audit builder for very long audits.
- Fixed several crashes in the mobile app — on Android when moving between screens
  shortly after viewing images, when quickly double-tapping a resource, and in rare
  cases at launch on iOS — and reduced memory pressure on older iPhones when opening
  a form with several video attachments.
- Fixed an issue where videos on Android showed an error after a brief network drop
  instead of resuming on their own.
- Fixed an issue where the "Check-ins to facilitate" row disappeared from a manager's
  home screen when nothing was pending, making it hard to get back to.
