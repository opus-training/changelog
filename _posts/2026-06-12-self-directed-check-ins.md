---
title: "Self-directed check-ins"
date: 2026-06-12
kind: feature
tags: [Feature, Check-ins]
---

A check-in can now be set up as self-directed, so a trainee works through it themselves.

- **Trainees complete it on their own** — the trainee works through the check-in themselves instead of a manager running the session with them.
- **Set per check-in** — choose self-directed when setting up a check-in.
