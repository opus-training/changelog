Question screens, the course introduction, and the completion screen have a cleaner, more consistent look on mobile.

- **Consistent question screens** — multiple choice, true/false, select all, matching, ordering, image choice, and written answers all share a cleaner look.
- **Inline introduction** — the course introduction now sits inline in the training flow.
- **Redesigned completion screen** — clearer pass and fail messaging, a direct link to the certificate, and a simpler way to leave feedback.
