title: "Deeper questions in your AI assistant"
date: 2026-09-04
kind: feature
tags: [Feature, Integrations, Reporting]
---

Your AI assistant can now answer questions that span your entire organization — completion rates by location, who holds which certification, how a team's training is trending — the kind of question that used to mean building a report or exporting a spreadsheet.

- **Ask across all of it at once.** Instead of looking things up one record at a time, the assistant can now total, compare and group your data to answer directly: which locations are furthest behind, how this quarter compares to last, how many people are still missing a required course.
- **Always read-only, always just your organization.** The assistant can read your data for these answers but can never change anything through them, and your organization's data is separated at the database itself — no other organization's data can be reached, and yours can't be reached by anyone else's.
- **Ask in plain language.** You describe what you want to know; the assistant works out how to get it. There's nothing new to set up — if you've already connected Opus to your assistant, it's there.

*Available to Admins with Opus connected to an AI assistant.*
