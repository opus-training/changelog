If you have already connected Opus to Claude, ChatGPT, or another AI assistant with a connector, it can now answer questions that span your whole business: completion rates by location, who holds which certification, how training is trending. That used to mean building a report or exporting a spreadsheet.

- **Ask across all of it at once.** Instead of looking things up one record at a time, the assistant can total, compare and group your data: which locations are furthest behind, how this quarter compares to last, how many people are still missing a required course.
- **Always read-only, always just your business.** The assistant can read your data for these answers but cannot change anything through them. It cannot see another business, and nobody else's assistant can see yours.
- **Ask in plain language.** You describe what you want to know; the assistant works out how to get it. There is nothing new to set up. If Opus is already connected to your assistant, it is there.

To set it up, add `https://api.opus.so/mcp` as a connector in your assistant and approve the connection once.

Learn more: [Getting Started: Opus MCP](https://help.opus.so/en/articles/16531461-getting-started-opus-mcp)

*Available to Admins with Opus connected to Claude, ChatGPT, or another AI assistant.*
