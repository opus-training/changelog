Put text on your training videos and smooth the cuts between clips, right in the video editor.

- **Add captions** — place text on the video for labels, quantities, or key steps, and drag it on the timeline to move or retime it.
- **Style them your way** — pick a font, color, size, position, and background, and choose how each caption fades, rises, pops, or types in.
- **Transition between clips** — fade through black, dissolve, or wipe from one clip to the next, and fade the video in and out.
- **See what you'll get** — the preview matches the saved video, and captions become part of the picture. Captions aren't translated, and subtitles are still created automatically after you save.
