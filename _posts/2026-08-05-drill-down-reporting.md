---
title: "Drill-down reporting"
date: 2026-08-05
kind: feature
tags: [Feature, Reporting]
---

Managers who oversee several locations can now see training reach and engagement grouped by the regions, brands, and franchises they are responsible for, and drill into any one of them.

- **Start with the big picture** — the reach and engagement table rolls up to the groups you manage instead of opening as one flat list of every location.
- **Drill down to a single location** — expand a group to compare the locations inside it and find the ones that need attention.
- **Consistent down the page** — reporting now groups the same way from the top of the page to the completion grid below it, so the numbers line up as you scroll.

*Managers who belong to a region, brand, or franchise see the grouped view; managers whose locations are not grouped see the same list as before.*
