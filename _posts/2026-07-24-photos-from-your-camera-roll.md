---
title: "Photos from your camera roll"
date: 2026-07-24
kind: feature
tags: [Feature, Mobile]
---

When a check-in, task list or audit asks for a photo or video, you can now pick one you already have.

- **Use what you've got** — pick an existing photo or video instead of only taking a new one on the spot.
- **Works across forms** — applies wherever a check-in, task list or audit asks someone for a photo or video.
