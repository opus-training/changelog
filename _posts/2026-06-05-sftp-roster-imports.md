---
title: "Roster imports from your provider's SFTP server"
date: 2026-06-05
kind: feature
tags: [Feature, Integrations]
---

Payroll and HR systems that host their own SFTP server, such as Paycom, can now be connected directly.

- **Connect self-hosted providers** — works with payroll and HR systems that host their own SFTP server.
- **Opus pulls the file for you** — the employee file is pulled on a schedule, instead of requiring the file to be sent to Opus first.
