Set up Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini with Opus from one place in Settings, and see who in your organization has connected an AI assistant and how much they use it.

- **Step-by-step setup.** Settings → AI connections has your Opus connector URL ready to copy, plus setup steps for Claude, ChatGPT and Gemini Enterprise.
- **See who's connected.** A list of everyone in your organization with a connected assistant, which assistant it is, and when they connected.
- **See how it's used.** When each connection was last used, and how many times the assistant has looked up or changed something in Opus.

*Available to Admins who can change organization settings.*
