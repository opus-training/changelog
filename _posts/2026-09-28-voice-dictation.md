---
title: "Voice dictation in AI prompts"
date: 2026-09-28
kind: feature
tags: [Feature, Ask Opus]
---

Speak your question or prompt instead of typing it, and Opus turns it into text.

- **Talk to Ask Opus** — tap the microphone in Ask Opus on the web or in the mobile app, ask your question out loud, and send it.
- **Build with your voice** — dictate into the AI prompts you use to create courses, suggest screens, and generate or edit images in the dashboard.
- **See that it's listening** — a live waveform shows while you record, with buttons to cancel or stop. Stop and send in one tap, or edit the text before you send it.
- **Up to two minutes at a time** — your words are added to whatever you've already typed.

*In the mobile app, update to the latest version if you don't see the microphone yet.*
