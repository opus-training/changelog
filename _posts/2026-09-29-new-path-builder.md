Paths are easier to build, and it's easier to see how people are moving through them.

- **Progress at a glance** — see who's in a Path, which step they're on, and when they started and finished.
- **Build at your own pace** — work on a draft, make changes, and publish when it's ready.
- **Stay organized** — filter, search, duplicate, and archive Paths from one place.
- **Premium modules in Paths** — add premium modules to a Path next to your own content.

*Available to all organizations as an opt-in: Admins can turn it on for their whole organization from the banner on the Paths page. It becomes the default for everyone next week.*
