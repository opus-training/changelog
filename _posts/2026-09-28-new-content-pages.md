date: 2026-09-28

tags: [Feature, Dashboard]

Open any content in the dashboard and see how your team is doing with it right away.

- **Progress on the first screen** — completion, scores, or submissions show as soon as you open content, without clicking through to Manage or Reporting.
- **Look without the risk of editing** — content opens read-only, and nothing changes until you click Edit.
- **See what Trainees see** — a phone preview on the page shows the content the way Trainees get it, and "Used in" lists everywhere it's used before you make a change.

*Available to Admins and Managers as an opt-in: click Try it at the bottom left of any content page. It turns on just for you and becomes the default for everyone in October.*
