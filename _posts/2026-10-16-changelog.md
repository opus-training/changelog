Ask Opus now answers quick searches, Opus Agents get more done, and fixes across the course builder, tables, checklists and the app.

### Improvements

- **Ask Opus answers searches.** Type a word or name, like "ranch" or "dress code", and Ask Opus replies with the key fact from your materials instead of asking you to rephrase. Ask a full question or say "full recipe" for the whole answer. It also finds exact names and codes that appear in your materials.
- **Opus Agents get more done.** Agents answer new-hire and overdue questions correctly, tell you which content mentions a topic (premium items included), act on Guest Feedback recommendations, fill in the Checklist or Check-in draft you have open, and take a file you pick when they ask for one.
- **Preview premium content before you buy.** Previewing a premium catalog Module or Course now opens its lessons, so you can see what you'd get.
- **Full names on hover.** Long step and screen names in the Module builder outline and the trainee preview show in full when you hover over them.

### Bug fixes

- Fixed an issue where the Ask Opus chat history list was empty, so you couldn't reopen past chats.
- Fixed an issue where table search boxes, such as on the HRIS Employees page, lost focus after each letter you typed.
- Fixed an issue where a PDF turned back from an Opus Doc offered Export PDF instead of Download.
- Fixed an issue where the view and zoom controls in the course builder covered the Cancel and Submit buttons on AI suggestions.
- Fixed small course builder issues: pressing Done in the crop tool could reset your crop, and the "add screen" tile sat higher than the screens next to it.
- Fixed an issue where the app showed a different part of a zoomed-in course image than the author framed in the builder.
- Fixed an issue where a Giphy GIF didn't show in the app when its video couldn't play.
- Fixed an issue where scheduled checklist slots at locations in western time zones disappeared from to-do lists hours before they closed.
