---
title: "Course generation follows your outline"
date: 2026-07-17
kind: feature
tags: [Feature, Courses]
---

When you build an outline screen by screen and generate the course, Opus now reproduces what you wrote rather than rewriting it.

- **Your words, kept** — the generated course reproduces what you wrote instead of rewriting it.
- **Briefs are requirements** — the instructions in your brief are treated as requirements instead of suggestions.
