Guest Feedback now opens on an Issues view built for working through problems rather than reading reviews one by one.

- **Work by issue, not by review** — filter by status or theme, sort the list, and see when each issue was first detected.
- **Redesigned detail page** — open each issue on a detail page with a clear next step.
- **Simpler setup** — connect a data source by linking a Google account or forwarding your Ovation review emails.
