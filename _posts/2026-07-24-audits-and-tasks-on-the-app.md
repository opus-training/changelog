The app's home screen is reorganized so people can see what they owe at a glance.

- **Clear home sections** — training, tasks and what someone was recently working on each have their own section.
- **Collapsed audit sections** — audits open with their sections collapsed, which makes a long audit much easier to work through.
- **Redesigned audit detail** — the audit detail screen has been redesigned.
