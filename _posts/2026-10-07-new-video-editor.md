date: 2026-10-07

Edit training videos right in the dashboard, then save the result in place of the original.

- **Trim and join** — cut out mistakes, split clips, freeze a frame, change speed, and add more clips from your computer or your Library.
- **Add a voiceover** — type a script and Opus creates the voice, or record your own over the video.
- **Add music** — generate background music from a mood or a description, or upload your own track. It gets quieter while the voiceover plays.
- **Pick up where you left off** — edits save as a draft while you work, and the original stays in use until you click Save video.

*Available for uploaded videos up to 10 minutes long in Courses, Modules, Check-ins, and Resources.*
