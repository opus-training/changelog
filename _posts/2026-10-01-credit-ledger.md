See exactly where your premium content credits go, entry by entry, from the new Credit ledger tab in Billing.

- **Every credit spend in one list** — see who was assigned which premium module, at which location, and how many credits it used, for the current billing period or any dates you pick.
- **Find what you need fast** — search, filter by people, location, or role, and sort any column.
- **See the big picture** — the summary breaks down spend by location, franchise, or role.
- **Export for your records** — download the ledger as an Excel file with your filters applied.

*Available to people who manage billing. The Credit ledger replaces the Credit spend tab.*
