Every organization now has a Recommendations page that turns the guest feedback you collect into specific training actions.

- **Themes from your reviews** — Opus groups recurring comments into themes and recommends what to do about each one.
- **From recommendation to course** — turn a recommendation straight into a course.
- **Faster analysis** — new reviews are analyzed within minutes of arriving instead of waiting for the weekly run, and automatic syncing is included for everyone.
