---
title: "Proof on imported certificates"
date: 2026-10-05
kind: feature
tags: [Feature, Reporting]
---

Certificates your team earned outside Opus can now carry their proof: attach an image or a link to any imported compliance certificate.

- **Upload or link.** Attach a PNG or JPG of the certificate, or paste a link to where it's hosted. You can replace or remove it later.
- **Add links in bulk.** Certificate import CSVs take an optional certificate link column, so proof arrives with the import.
- **Open it anywhere.** Imported certificates with proof can be viewed and downloaded from compliance reporting and a person's certificates, and open with a tap in the mobile app.

*Available to Admins who can manage premium content or certificate imports.*
