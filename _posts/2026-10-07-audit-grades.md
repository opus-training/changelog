title: "Audit grades"
date: 2026-10-07
kind: feature
tags: [Feature, Audits]
---

Grade every Audit and each of its sections on a scale you set, from Excellent to Needs improvement.

- **Your ranges, your names** — start from four grades, then rename them, change their colors and starting percentages, or add more.
- **A grade for every section** — each submission gets an overall grade and a grade for each section, so you can see where a Location fell short.
- **Grades everywhere results show** — on the submission in the dashboard and the app, in the Audit's Reporting tab, the Audit Completion Grid, and exports.
- **Past results stay put** — changing an Audit's scoring applies when you publish and never changes the grade on past submissions.

*Available to organizations using Audits. Set it from Scoring on an Audit's Overview tab.*
