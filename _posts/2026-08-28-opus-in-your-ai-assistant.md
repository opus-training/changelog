title: "Opus in your AI assistant"
date: 2026-08-28

tags: [Feature, Integrations]
---

Connect Opus to the AI assistant you already use — Claude, ChatGPT, or anything else that supports connectors — and ask it about your training, your team and your locations, or have it draft content for you.

- **Ask about your own data in plain English** — who's past due, how each location is tracking, what's in your content library, who's on your team. The assistant reads live from Opus, so the answer reflects your account as it stands right now.
- **Have it draft your content** — courses, check-ins, checklists, audits and documents arrive as drafts in your library, ready for you to review, edit and publish.
- **It can only do what you can** — a connection acts as you, so a manager sees and changes only their own locations, exactly as in the dashboard. When you connect, you choose read-only or read-and-write, and the approval screen spells out which one you're granting.
- **Access follows the account** — if someone is deactivated or loses admin or manager access, their connection stops working immediately.

To set it up, add `https://api.opus.so/mcp` as a connector in your assistant and approve the connection once.

*Available to admins and managers.*
