date: 2026-07-24

When an audit turns up something that needs fixing, you can now track the fix instead of chasing it by hand.

- **Owner, due date, status** — each follow-up task moves from not started, through in progress and ready for review, to completed, and you can require a photo as proof before it is signed off.
- **A new Tasks page** — everything open across your locations in one list, and each task has its own page with comments, mentions and a full history of what happened.
- **Nothing left behind** — anything you already had open in the previous remediation flow has been carried over, with its comments and history intact.

*Available to organizations using audits.*
