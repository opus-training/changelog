date: 2026-07-03
kind: feature
tags: [Feature, Reporting]

The Billing page now shows how many premium content credits each of your locations used over the current billing period.

- **Per-location breakdown** — a new Credit Spend by Location table on the Billing page in Settings.
- **Stable attribution** — spend is counted under the location a team member joined first, so someone who has transferred stores stays with their original location.
